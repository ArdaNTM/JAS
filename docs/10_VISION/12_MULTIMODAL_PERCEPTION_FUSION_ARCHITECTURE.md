# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Multimodal Perception Fusion Architecture responsible for transforming independent sensory observations into a unified, coherent, temporally consistent understanding of the external world.

Rather than treating vision, speech, browser activity, system telemetry, environmental sensors, documents, memory and agent observations as isolated inputs, JAS SHALL combine them into one synchronized perception model that continuously updates the internal World Model.

The objective is to enable JAS to perceive reality similarly to how humans integrate multiple senses simultaneously.

# 3. Design Philosophy

No modality is considered absolutely correct.

Every observation contributes evidence.

Reality emerges from evidence fusion.

Temporal consistency is more important than instantaneous certainty.

Independent sensors increase confidence.

Conflicting observations require reasoning rather than immediate rejection.

Fusion must remain explainable.
