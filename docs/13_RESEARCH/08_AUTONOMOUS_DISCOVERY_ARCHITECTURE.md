# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Autonomous Discovery Architecture (ADA), the subsystem responsible for enabling JAS to independently identify unknown information, discover knowledge gaps, generate research objectives, explore solution spaces, and continuously expand its understanding of the world.

Autonomous Discovery transforms JAS from a passive information processor into an active intelligence system capable of identifying what it does not know and initiating structured discovery processes.
