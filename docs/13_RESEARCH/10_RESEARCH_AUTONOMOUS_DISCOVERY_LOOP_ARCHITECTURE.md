# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Autonomous Discovery Loop Architecture (ADLA), the subsystem responsible for enabling JAS to continuously identify unknown areas, discover missing knowledge, formulate research objectives, execute exploration processes, and improve its own understanding.

The objective of this architecture is to transform JAS from a passive research assistant into an active intelligence system capable of autonomous knowledge expansion.

---

# 2. Objectives

The Autonomous Discovery Loop SHALL provide:

- Unknown knowledge detection
- Research opportunity identification
- Autonomous hypothesis generation
- Exploration planning
- Knowledge gap analysis
- Research prioritization
- Discovery feedback integration
- Continuous intelligence improvement
