# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Research Knowledge Synthesis Architecture (RKSA), the subsystem responsible for transforming discovered information, research results, experimental outputs, and validated evidence into structured, reusable, and continuously improving knowledge models.

The purpose of this architecture is to ensure that JAS does not merely collect information but converts information into integrated understanding.

---

# 2. Objectives

The Research Knowledge Synthesis subsystem SHALL provide:

- Evidence aggregation
- Knowledge consolidation
- Concept relationship creation
- Research result integration
- Contradiction resolution
- Understanding refinement
- Knowledge model expansion
- Continuous learning support
