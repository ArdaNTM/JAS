# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Knowledge Synthesis Architecture (KSA), responsible for transforming validated evidence into higher-level knowledge structures that can be consumed by reasoning, planning, memory, coding, autonomous agents, and future learning systems.

Knowledge synthesis is not simple summarization. It is the process of discovering relationships, abstractions, causal structures, generalized principles, reusable concepts, hypotheses, and predictive models from multiple evidence sources.

---

# 2. Objectives

The architecture SHALL:

- Convert evidence into knowledge
- Discover hidden relationships
- Produce reusable abstractions
- Build conceptual models
- Generate generalized principles
- Identify causal mechanisms
- Detect missing knowledge
- Support autonomous learning
- Improve future reasoning quality
- Continuously refine knowledge quality

---

The architecture includes:

---

# 4. Architectural Philosophy

Knowledge SHALL always represent a higher abstraction than evidence.

Evidence answers:

"What happened?"

Knowledge answers:

"What does this mean?"

Wisdom answers:

"When should this knowledge be used?"

The Knowledge Synthesis layer operates between Evidence Fusion and Reasoning.
